Muffin Button

Because every stream needs more muffins.

What is it?

A tiny OBS Browser Source that throws random muffins onto your stream whenever someone presses the button or says muffin in Twitch chat.

Muffins have physics, bouncing, collisions, spinning and a satisfying muffin sound.

OBS Setup

Add a Browser Source to OBS and point it at the Muffin Button page.

You can configure it directly through the URL:

?channel=YOUR_CHANNEL&min=1&max=5&button=false

URL Options

channel — Twitch channel

min / max — muffins per trigger

button=false — hide the manual button

gravity — muffin gravity

bounce — floor/wall bounce

lifetime — how long muffins remain

maxMuffins — maximum muffins on screen

Made for absolutely necessary muffin-related streaming purposes.
